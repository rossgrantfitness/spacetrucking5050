  dialogue box, button and sign.
- **Controls:** the start screen is clean now (a small "test your keys and
  gamepad" link opens the old input check). **Esc (Start) > CONTROLS**
  lists every key and button, flying, on foot and anywhere, keyboard and
  gamepad side by side.
