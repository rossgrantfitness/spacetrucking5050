## What works (built and tested)

- **Flying:**
  - arcade "airplane in space" handling, throttle lever with an idle notch;
  - boost with speed wobbles, overdrive and hull strain;
  - momentum and mass from cargo weight;
  - chase cam that looks all the way around the rig, plus cockpit and cinema cameras;
  - space dust, FOV, speed lines, trails and engine hum.
- **The delivery loop:** job boards and job offers, course chart and autopilot, approach rings and auto-dock, payouts, fuel, bonks, cargo condition, care and rush bonuses.
- **The world:** three systems (home, Tidewater, Glimmer), four stations, asteroid shells and traffic lanes, landmarks, route events and sights from your list (the ones needing new models or systems are still to come), traffic, a logbook.
- **On foot:**
  - the rig is home: cabin, hallway, galley, cargo bay, engine room, dispatch;
  - the truck stop, the cannery canteen, the casino lounge;
  - fixed FF8-style cameras, crew with daily routines, vending machines with 12 brands.
- **Economy and time:** a 365-day calendar, weekly bills, a tab, insurance, upgrades on shelves, 14 rigs, paint, rig XP.
- **Story so far:** the opening (Raccoony, the wheel, Marge), Marge's first job, Sal's three-job casino storyline, the objective line with "!" markers.
- **Look and sound:**
  - PS1 shaders, dithering, low-res, per-system fog;
  - 21 radio stations (placeholder music);
  - pleasant UI sounds, voice blips.
- **Comfort:**
  - window and fullscreen, remembered;
  - menu size, invert Y, camera roll, shake on/off, rumble, HUD on/off;
  - three volume sliders, watch mode.
- **Testing:** the F10 debug menu (jump near any station, give any job).

## Tech debt (what would slow the next steps)

1. **`FlightSandbox.gd` is 1,500 lines.** It handles docking, the cabin, naps, the opening, overdrive warnings, the debug jump, the horn, the docking computer and more. Before adding systems or the company check-ins, I'll move a few self-contained pieces into their own small scripts. This is the one refactor that unblocks the next tasks.
2. **Some words live in code instead of data:**
   - about 90 lines of player-facing text, mostly menu text in `HubServices.gd`, the objective lines and a few comm lines;
   - the brief wants content in data;
   - the story and company work will put new dialogue in data files, and move existing lines as they're touched.
3. **The save format is still "version 1".** The loader tolerates missing fields, so old saves still load. The company work changes how pay works, so I'll bump the version and convert old saves.
4. **Builder tools can overwrite hand edits.** Rooms and models are generated by `tools/build_*.gd`. If one is re-run after a scene was edited by hand, the edit is lost. Rule: never re-run a builder without checking `git diff` after.
5. **Validation takes about 30 minutes**, mostly the play-throughs. Fine for now; if it grows much more I'll split it into quick and full.
6. **No export presets yet** (task 6): there's no Windows build to hand to anyone.
7. **Options missing from the brief:** shake amount (only on/off), FOV, and a separate "radio-off music" volume (task 6).
8. **Reference files:** `reference/` holds a few `.mp3` tracks and other people's images. They're never shipped with the game (Godot ignores the folder), but they're in the git repo. If the repo ever goes public, remove any music you don't have rights to.
